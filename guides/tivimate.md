# TiviMate Setup Guide
This guide explains how to configure TiviMate with your subscription.

## Requirements
- Internet connection
- TiviMate app
- Your subscription credentials

## Step 1
Download TiviMate from the Google Play Store for your Android or Fire TV device.

## Step 2
Open the application, select Add Playlist, and choose Xtream Codes login or the method provided by your service.

## Step 3
Enter your server URL, username, and password, then click Next to complete the setup.

## Troubleshooting
- Check your internet connection.
- Verify your login credentials.
- Restart the application if needed.
