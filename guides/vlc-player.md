VLC Media Player Setup Guide
This guide explains how to configure VLC Media Player with your subscription.

Requirements
Internet connection

VLC Media Player app

Your subscription M3U URL

Step 1
Download VLC Media Player from the official application store or website for your device.

Step 2
Open the application, navigate to the Media menu (or Network tab on mobile), and select Open Network Stream.

Step 3
Paste your M3U playlist URL provided by your service into the network URL field, then click Play to load your playlist.

Troubleshooting
Check your internet connection.

Verify your playlist URL for typos.

Restart the application if needed.
