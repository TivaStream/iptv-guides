# IPTV Smarters Setup Guide

This guide explains how to configure IPTV Smarters with your subscription.

## Requirements

- Internet connection
- IPTV Smarters app
- Your subscription credentials

## Step 1

Download IPTV Smarters from the official application store for your device.

## Step 2

Open the application and choose **Login with Xtream Codes API** or the method provided by your service.

## Step 3

Enter your account details and complete the setup.

## Troubleshooting

- Check your internet connection.
- Verify your login credentials.
- Restart the application if needed.
