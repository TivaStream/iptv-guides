IPTV Buffering Fix Guide
This guide explains how to fix buffering, lag, and freezing issues on your IPTV service.

Requirements
Active internet connection (minimum 25 Mbps recommended)

Your streaming device (Firestick, Android TV, Smart TV, etc.)

A high-performance IPTV player (e.g., TiviMate, XCIPTV)

Step 1
Switch from Wi-Fi to a wired Ethernet connection, or move your streaming device closer to your Wi-Fi router to ensure signal stability.

Step 2
Clear the cache of your IPTV app by going to your device's Settings > Applications > [Your IPTV App] > Clear Cache.

Step 3
Reboot your router and streaming device to clear temporary network memory and refresh your connection.

Troubleshooting
Turn on a reliable VPN to bypass potential ISP throttling.

Change the video decoder setting inside your IPTV app from Hardware to Software (or vice versa).

Lower the stream output resolution if your connection speed drops during peak hours.
