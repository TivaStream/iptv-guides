# XCIPTV Setup Guide
This guide explains how to configure XCIPTV with your subscription.

## Requirements
- Internet connection
- XCIPTV app
- Your subscription credentials

## Step 1
Download XCIPTV Player from the official app store for your Android or Fire TV device.

## Step 2
Open the application to reach the main login screen.

## Step 3
Enter your XCIPTV credentials (Server URL, Username, and Password) provided by your service, then click Sign In to complete the setup.

## Troubleshooting
- Check your internet connection.
- Verify your login credentials.
- Restart the application if needed.
