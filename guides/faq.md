# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

This guide addresses common questions regarding IPTV configuration, compatible media players, and streaming issues.

## Requirements :
- Stable internet connection
- Compatible IPTV player app (TiviMate, XCIPTV, VLC, etc.)
= Active IPTV subscription credentials

## Frequently Asked Questions :
- What format is required to load my playlist?
Most IPTV players support either Xtream Codes API (Server URL, Username, Password) or M3U Playlist URLs.

- Why is my channel list not updating?
Force close your application and clear its cache, or manually trigger a playlist update within your IPTV player's playlist settings.

- Do I need a VPN for IPTV streaming?
A VPN is recommended if your Internet Service Provider (ISP) throttles high-bandwidth streaming traffic or blocks specific IPTV servers.

## Troubleshooting :
- Verify that your subscription credentials or M3U link are active.
- Ensure your internet speed meets the minimum 25 Mbps requirement for HD/4K streams.
- Restart your router and streaming device to resolve connection timeouts.
